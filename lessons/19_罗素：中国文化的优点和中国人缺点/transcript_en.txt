After I talked about the reason why Chinese culture can live long last time, we conducted an overall scan of Chinese culture. During the overall scan, I cannot but thank an Englishman named Russell. He changed my attitude towards the Chinese culture that I am based on. I must mention him. Russell came to Hong Kong, Shanghai, Hangzhou, Nanjing, Changsha, and finally Beijing during the worst time in China (1920), where he stayed for a full nine months. Now that I think about it, China at that time was really in ruins, with nothing going for it, foreign powers everywhere, and living in unparalleled poverty.
Russell spent nine months in China, mainly in Beijing, where he delivered five formal speeches. What he talks about is all about his profession. I wonder what the people listening below hope to hear from the British about how to save the country? Because I looked at his topics, one was "Mathematical Logic", another was "Analysis of Objects", another was "Analysis of Mind", another was "Philosophical Issues", and the last one was called "Social Structure". In addition to these lectures, he also gave some lectures on idealism, causality, relativity, gravity, etc. Regardless of whether Russell's speech was understood or not, the young people in China at that time wanted to change China's reality, so there were many people who listened to it, and the scene was very lively. Among Russell's audience were the young Mao Zedong and Zhou Enlai.
Russell stayed in China for nine months. Later he said: "After lecturing in China, I found more and more that I learned much more from China than China learned from me. I asked many Western scholars who had been lecturing in China for a long time. They had the same point of view as me. What point of view? That is, we can learn much more from China." Please note that this is a very remarkable kind of thinking, because China was in ruins everywhere at that time.
Chinese cultural confidant
When Russell published "The China Question" in 1922, he said at the beginning, "In the next two centuries, whether China gets better or worse, it will have a decisive impact on the world." So what is the decisive impact? Russell made a very important statement. He said, "Whether China or the world, culture is the most important. As long as cultural problems can be solved, no matter what political and economic system China adopts, I will accept it." I want to read this sentence again. He said, "No matter China or the world, culture is the most important. As long as cultural problems can be solved, no matter what political system and economic system China adopts, I will accept it." So because of this high level, Russell has become a confidant of Chinese culture.
I have read many works on Chinese culture by Western scholars, such as Breinitz, Goethe, Voltaire, Hegel, Weber, and Spengler. Some have high evaluations, some have low evaluations, but the one I feel most intimate with is Russell. You see, he said this sentence, and my heart moved when I read it. He said, "Progress and efficiency have made us rich and powerful, but they have been ignored by the Chinese. But before we harassed them, they returned to peace and prosperity." I shed tears when I read it. He said: "Progress and efficiency have made us rich and powerful, but they have been ignored by the Chinese. But before we harass them, they will return to peace and prosperity." This is the language of an intimate, an understanding of the overall appearance of Chinese culture, and an international-level master.
How did he come to these conclusions? He came into contact with many specific Chinese people. For example, one day in the early summer of 1921, Russell, accompanied by a group of Chinese friends, went hiking in a sedan chair. At that time, the road was not easy to walk at all, so we had to take a sedan chair. The weather was very hot that day, and the heat came very suddenly, and the mountain road was very rugged, so the bearers worked very hard. Finally, they climbed to the highest peak, and everyone took a rest. These Chinese bearers sat in a row, took out their pipes, smoked happily, and joked with each other, as if the foreign affairs of the world had no concern at all in their laughter. Russell looked at them and thought, if this happened in another country, and the weather suddenly became very hot without warning today, the bearers would have to pay more because of the unbearable heat and the rugged mountain roads. Even a European like me will not be without worries when we reach the mountain, but will definitely worry about what will happen to the transportation after going down the mountain. But Chinese people make fun of people whenever they have free time. This is really another culture.
Thinking about it, he said: "No matter where I go, the Europeans will first tell you the transportation facilities and practical information of the place. But what the Chinese tell you is definitely not the most practical thing. He will tell you that there was a poet who lived here in ancient times." Of course Russell cannot simply judge such things, who is good or bad, but he feels fairly that Western civilization is not the only standard. Let me read the following passage, and everyone listens:
"Caucasians have a strong desire to dominate others, but Chinese people have the virtue of not wanting to dominate other countries. It is this virtue that makes China appear weak internationally. In fact, if there is a country in the world that proudly disdains war, this country is China. If China is willing, it can become the most powerful nation in the world."
Russell said what I once said at the United Nations Conference on World Civilizations, that is, China's non-aggressive nature. But Russell, because he was an outsider, could step away and speak in a humorous way. If I say it this way, it would seem too proud and excessive, so I have to read Russell's passage again. He said:
"Caucasians have a strong desire to dominate others, but Chinese people have the virtue of not wanting to dominate other countries. It is this virtue that makes China appear weak internationally. In fact, if there is a country in the world that proudly disdains war, this country is China. If China is willing, it can become the most powerful nation in the world."
This is what Russell said. He believed that China was weak because it did not want to invade. But if China wants, it can become the most powerful nation in the world. Because of pride, pride in their own culture, they are proud and do not want to fight.
Disadvantages of the Chinese
Next, I would like to talk about some of his various analyzes of China. He said: "China is one of the best countries I have ever come into contact with. Now it is so mistreated by the foreign powers. I issue the most serious protest against these foreign powers. The Chinese also have shortcomings. Because they are not soft on us, I do not want to pinch their shortcomings. This is for the sake of truth and for China. I cannot hide it. When I left China, a Chinese scholar asked me to point out the main weaknesses of the Chinese people. I talked about three points. Not only were they not angry, but they thought my judgment was more appropriate."
Russell believes that Chinese people have three common shortcomings. The first shortcoming is greed, the second shortcoming is timidity, and the third shortcoming is indifference. He regards greed as the number one shortcoming of the Chinese people. He said that except for a few people, many Chinese people are corrupt whenever they have the opportunity, and even dare to take risks for a small amount of money. Acceptance of bribes by political figures has become one of the reasons why China cannot effectively resist foreign powers. He kindly hopes that when China's economy develops, its moral standards will also improve.
Russell believes that the second shortcoming of the Chinese is timidity. The Chinese can be passively patient and lack active courage. In war, both sides are often not serious, and the army is defeated without much fighting. He said that this was simply unimaginable to the reputation-loving Europeans. But after Russell said this, he immediately added: "I have heard that this kind of war is a melee between warlords, and it is understandable that people do not work hard. If the Chinese encounter a real catastrophe, they will still be brave." This is the second shortcoming of the Chinese people mentioned by Russell.
The third shortcoming of the Chinese people mentioned by Russell is called indifference. He said that Chinese people lack humanitarian impulse and rarely do charity and disaster relief. What is even more unforgivable is that if a cute dog is hit by a car on the streets of China, most passers-by will find it funny. Among civilians, other people's disasters tend to inspire pleasure rather than sympathy.
In addition to these three points, Russell also analyzed some other problems in China, such as the lack of motivation and efficiency, the weakness and chaos of the judiciary, the lack of face, etc. By the time he left, he had deeply felt the powerlessness caused by the inertia of China's reformers in trying to change China, and from this he felt an inescapable sense of helplessness among hundreds of millions of Chinese. But until this time, he still had a great appreciation for the fundamentals of Chinese culture.
Russell talked about the advantages of Chinese culture and the shortcomings of the Chinese people. This made me feel very strongly. Okay, thank you, I'll talk about it next time.

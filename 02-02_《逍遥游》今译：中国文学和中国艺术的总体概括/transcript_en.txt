Nothing to rely on
Everyone, what I just read was "Xiaoyaoyou". I have skipped some passages in the modern translation of it, and some of the more difficult passages have been skipped. After it turned into a bird, it flew thousands of miles away, hit the water for three thousand miles, and soared into the sky for ninety thousand miles. The way he flies like this is very beautiful, both in the sky and on the ground. However, when I was flying, there suddenly appeared chilling cicadas and small doves. Even small insects and small birds laughed underground. What's the use of you? We have also flown up and over, and when we can’t fly anymore, we just come back. Why fly 90,000 miles?
Zhuangzi sighed, it was such a small thing that he didn't know how big it was. But what he means is that when he was young, he cited people like Song Rongzi. He felt that this person was great. He would not be motivated by the praise from the world; he would not be depressed by the criticism from the world. However, people like Song Rongzi, including Liezi, are like this. Their relaxation and happiness are already great, but they still need to rely on something, such as relying on the wind. Those who fly in the sky also need to rely on the wind. If there is no wind, they will not be free.
What he means by "Xiaoyaoyou" is that you don't need to rely on anything. This is the original intention of Xiaoyaoyou. He came to the conclusion that the perfect man, the supreme being, does not need to guard himself or defend himself; the god does not need merit, merit is what I rely on; the saint does not need fame, and fame is also what I rely on.
